About the GSSO Ontology

Origins, design methodology, publications, and citation guide.

Project Background

The Gender, Sex, and Sexual Orientation (GSSO) ontology is a comprehensive terminology designed to represent concepts across gender identity, gender expression, romantic orientation, sexual orientation, health conditions, clinical care, and social interactions. Its focus is on maintaining a multidisciplinary and intersectional approach.

The project originally hosted a stand-alone web resource at Cincinnati Children's Hospital and Medical Center (CCHMC). This modern GitHub Pages site serves as the official replacement portal, providing full term browsing, text annotation, and cross-reference lookups directly aligned with the open-source repository.
